# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. A whole new angle. Carom takes the quick, readable rhythm
of a paddle duel and gives it a twist: every moving strike bends the ball's
flight. Settle into the opening rally, find your timing, and turn a safe return
into a shot your opponent never saw coming.

The mint and coral paddles face off across a dark court, but the middle is
anything but empty. Two amber bars sway and rotate through the lanes. Catch a
tilted face for a bank shot, thread the gap, or let a curved return find its own
unexpected route. The luminous trail makes every change of direction readable.

Take on the machine in Solo, or pull up a second chair for a local Versus match.
The ball gets faster with every paddle hit, and a single well-timed sweep can
change the shape of the rally. There is room for careful placement and room for
glorious last-second saves.

First to eleven wins, but you need a two-point lead. At deuce, every angle
matters: hold your nerve, work the obstacles, and put a little spin on match
point.
